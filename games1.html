MathPath — Practice Math from Counting to Calculus

MathPath is a free K-12 mathematics learning platform built around short video lessons, downloadable worksheets, and adaptive problem sets that adjust to each learner's pace.

Subjects We Cover

Our curriculum spans arithmetic, fractions, decimals, ratios, percentages, pre-algebra, Algebra I and II, geometry, trigonometry, pre-calculus, single-variable calculus, statistics and probability, and discrete math. Every topic includes a concept overview, worked examples, and timed practice quizzes.

Grade Levels

Lessons are organized by grade band: K-2 number sense, 3-5 operations and fractions, 6-8 ratios and early algebra, and 9-12 high school sequences including honors and AP tracks. Homeschool families can switch between grade levels without losing progress.

Test Prep

Targeted prep modules are available for the SAT Math, ACT Math, PSAT, GRE Quantitative, ASVAB arithmetic, and state assessments such as STAAR, FSA, and Regents. Each module includes diagnostic tests and full-length timed practice exams with detailed answer explanations.

How Students Learn

Learners watch a five-minute concept video, complete ten guided practice problems, then attempt a mastery check. A spaced-repetition engine resurfaces missed concepts after one day, one week, and one month so skills stick long-term.

For Teachers and Parents

Free educator accounts include printable worksheet generators, answer keys, standards alignment to Common Core and TEKS, and class progress dashboards. Parents receive weekly email summaries of their child's practice activity.
